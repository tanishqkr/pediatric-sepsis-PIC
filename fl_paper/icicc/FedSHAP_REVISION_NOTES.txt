1. SUMMARY OF REVISION OBJECTIVES & RESOLUTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Item 1: Held-Out Test Evaluation vs. SHAP Background Reference
- Disclosed and clarified the explicit boundary between evaluation and explanation:
  * Model evaluation is conducted on the held-out test cohort of N = 633 stays
    (173 Phoenix-positive, 460 Phoenix-negative).
  * The SHAP GradientSHAP reference background was formed by taking a 100-patient
    sample drawn from B_test_model_ready.csv using random seed 42.
  * Explicitly acknowledged in Section III-D and Section V (Limitations) that
    sharing this centralized 100-sample background across local ICU nodes qualifies
    strict data residency guarantees.

Item 2: Centralized Baseline Rows in Table 2
- Removed Centralized FT-Transformer (AUROC 0.9577) and Centralized CatBoost
  (AUROC 0.9868) rows from Table 2 and all associated comparative narrative.
- Verification confirmed that these two values were hardcoded literals in Phase 7
  scripts without an autonomous, reproducible training pipeline in the federated
  setup. Removing them makes the paper self-contained and focused strictly on
  the empirical comparison between FedAvg and FedProx.

Item 3: Leave-One-Out (LOO) Interpretation and Ranking Concordance
- Revised Table 3 to present both self-inclusion Spearman rank correlations
  (rho = 0.9297 to 0.9739) and leave-one-out (LOO) correlations
  (rho = 0.9118 to 0.9669).
- Removed overclaims asserting that correlation p-values "prove clinical consensus".
  Reframed correlations as empirical evidence of ranking concordance across
  uneven care-unit sample sizes under the evaluated clinical partition.
- Clarified that the small asymptotic p-values (p < 10^-4) are statistical
  properties of rank permutations over D = 20 feature dimensions, not clinical
  validation.

Item 4: CICU Clinical Explanations Framed as Hypotheses
- Section IV-B has been revised to clarify that post-cardiac surgery coagulopathy,
  cardiopulmonary bypass hemodilution, and platelet consumption are plausible
  clinical hypotheses consistent with intensive care literature, rather than
  experimentally confirmed biological mechanisms.
- Disclosed that surgical procedure codes, intraoperative perfusion records,
  and clinical notes were unavailable in the tabular PIC database.

Item 5: FedProx Reproducible Operating Threshold
- Table 2 and Section IV-A now explicitly report the full-precision post-hoc
  decision threshold t* = 0.0014035846 (rounded to t* ~= 0.00140 in narrative).
- Documented the exact test confusion matrix produced by this threshold:
  True Positives (TP) = 156, False Positives (FP) = 72,
  False Negatives (FN) = 17,  True Negatives (TN) = 388.
  Sensitivity = 156 / 173 = 90.17%, Specificity = 388 / 460 = 84.35%.
- Fully acknowledged that this threshold was obtained by sweeping the held-out
  test set itself to achieve >= 90% sensitivity, and represents a descriptive
  operating point rather than a prospectively validated clinical decision rule.

Item 6: Mathematical Explanation of CICU FedProx 1.0000 Metric
- Transparently documented in Section IV-A that the FedProx test AUROC/AUPRC of
  1.0000 on the Cardiac ICU (CICU) partition is a small-sample boundary
  phenomenon:
  * CICU test cohort consists of N = 40 stays (29 positive, 11 negative).
  * Predicted risk scores satisfied min(p_pos) = 0.000568 > max(p_neg) = 0.000353.
  * Because every positive case scored strictly higher than every negative case,
    the empirical AUROC is 1.0000, and all non-parametric bootstrap resamples
    yield [1.0000, 1.0000].
  * Clarified that this reflects complete rank separability on 11 negative
    controls rather than infallible prospective diagnostic accuracy.

2. COMPREHENSIVE LIMITATIONS AUDIT (SECTION V)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The manuscript explicitly states eight boundaries of validity:
1. Single-Center Care-Unit Simulation (PIC database, Zhejiang University).
2. Diagnostic Co-occurrence & Windowing (first-24-hour observation window).
3. Post-Hoc Decision Threshold Selection (swept directly on held-out test data).
4. Reference SHAP Background Workflow (100-sample test cohort background shared).
5. Absence of Formal Differential Privacy (cleartext attribution aggregation).
6. Centralized Preprocessing (pooled StandardScaler simulation shortcut).
7. Unexecuted Sensitivity Axes (cohort definition and Dirichlet partitions unrun).
8. Lack of Prospective Clinician Review (no bedside decision-impact study).
