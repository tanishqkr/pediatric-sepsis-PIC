# Scientific Claim Audit Table — ICICC 2027 Submission 359
**Paper Title:** Federated SHAP: Privacy-Preserving, Per-Node Explainability and Sensitivity Analysis for Pediatric Sepsis Prediction  
**Audit Conducted Against:** Submitted manuscript (`FedSHAP.tex` / `FedSHAP_cameraready.tex`), repository codebase, and primary literature.

## Claim-Audit Matrix

| # | Current Manuscript Claim | Evidence Actually Available | Problem or Qualification Needed | Recommended Replacement Wording | New Computation Required? |

| **1** | *"No published study combines federated training with federated explainability -- i.e., SHAP computed locally at each participating site and aggregated without any node's raw data or per-patient attributions ever leaving that site."* (Section I, lines 42–43) | Recent papers (Bonsignori et al., *IEEE Access* 2026; Ducange et al., *Machine Learning* 2026) have proposed federated SHAP aggregation and federated background generation. | Absolute novelty assertion is factually incorrect and challenged by Reviewer 1 (Comment 1). | *"While recent conceptual frameworks such as iFLASH (Bonsignori et al., 2026) and Federated SHAP (Ducange et al., 2026) have investigated distributed post-hoc explanations in synthetic or non-medical benchmarks, the end-to-end evaluation of per-node federated explainability under severe clinical class and volume imbalance in pediatric sepsis has not been systematically demonstrated."* | No. Resolved by literature review. |
| **2** | *"Data flow and boundary guarantees at explanation time... never transmits raw patient data or per-patient attributions off a node... federation solves privacy."* (Abstract, Section III-D, Table I) | Only mean absolute SHAP vector ($1\times225$) and sample size $n_k$ are sent. No differential privacy noise or cryptographic secure aggregation was implemented. | Reviewer 1 (Comment 2): Absence of raw record egress is not a formal privacy guarantee. Aggregate vectors from small nodes (e.g., CICU, $n=40$ test cases) are vulnerable to membership inference or reconstruction attacks (Shokri et al., 2021; Patel et al., 2022). | *"The architecture enforces strict data locality and architectural isolation: raw records and individual per-patient SHAP attributions remain entirely on-premise. However, this architectural separation does not constitute a formal mathematical differential privacy guarantee; summary vectors from smaller clinical cohorts may remain vulnerable to inference unless augmented with secure aggregation or differential privacy mechanisms."* | No. Resolved by rigorous threat modeling and qualification. |
| **3** | Claims implying multi-hospital or cross-institutional generalization: *"trustworthy across heterogeneous clinical sites... toward a deployable multi-site system."* (Abstract, Section I, Conclusion) | All 5 simulated nodes (CICU, NICU, PICU, SICU, General) are derived from a single center's database (PIC database v1.1.0, Zhejiang University). | Reviewer 1 (Comment 3): Intra-database care-unit partitioning does not establish cross-hospital generalizability across different institutions, EHR systems, or clinical protocols. | Explicitly frame as a *simulation-based evaluation using care-unit partitions of a single pediatric intensive care database*: *"These findings demonstrate proof-of-concept explanation consistency across simulated ICU care units within a single healthcare center, serving as a lower bound on federated heterogeneity rather than evidence of cross-institutional generalizability across independent hospital networks."* | No. Resolved by qualification. |
| **4** | *"Feature values are standardized using a single StandardScaler fit on the pooled training distribution prior to federated partitioning, consistent with a realistic deployment..."* (Section III-A, line 71) | In the simulation script (`phase7_federated_learning.py`), `StandardScaler` was fitted centrally on the pooled `train_df`. | Reviewer 1 (Comment 3): Centralized pooling assumes access to global preprocessing statistics not directly available in a decentralized deployment without communication. | Clarify that in true decentralized FL, global mean and variance are obtained via a standard single-round federated sum aggregation (federated Welford's algorithm) without sharing patient-level data. Acknowledge this pooled fit as an exact computational proxy, but discuss local scaling heterogeneity as an operational consideration. | No. Resolved by methodological clarification. |
| **5** | *"GradientSHAP attributions were computed using the Captum (v0.7.0) interpretability library."* (Section III-E, line 111) | Code inspection reveals `import shap; explainer = shap.GradientExplainer(model, background)` from the official `shap` Python library, using $N=100$ background samples drawn from the test set with `random_state=42`. | Library mismatch and missing background specification (Reviewer 1, Comment 5). | State accurately that explanations were computed using `shap.GradientExplainer` (v0.42+) with an empirical reference background of $N=100$ samples selected via fixed random seed ($42$). | No. Resolved by documenting verifiable code configuration. |
| **6** | Explaining why sample-size weighting is used for explanations: *"aggregating them with the same sample-size weighting used for federated model updates..."* (Section III-B, Eq. 1) | The code uses $w_k = n_k / N$ identically to FedAvg model aggregation. No comparison with uniform weighting was provided in the text. | Reviewer 1 (Comment 1): FedAvg weights are derived from empirical loss minimization. Why should feature explanations follow the same weighting rather than uniform weighting or explanation stability? | Provide formal rationale: Sample-size weighting estimates population-level expected attribution $\mathbb{E}_{(x,y)\sim \mathcal{D}}[\|\phi(x)\|]$, whereas uniform weighting answers an institutional consensus question. Report newly computed empirical audit showing that sample-size and uniform weighting achieve near-identical ranking ($\rho = 0.9929$ for FedAvg, $\rho = 0.9955$ for FedProx, 100% top-5 overlap). Discuss stability-based weighting as an open direction. | No. Computed from existing node JSONs. |
| **7** | *"lactate_max being the unanimous top feature across all five nodes reinforces this reading... model has learned a genuine, generalizable physiological signal..."* (Section IV-B, Section V) | Sepsis is labelled using Phoenix Criteria (Schlapbach et al., 2024; Sanchez-Pinto et al., 2024), where the cardiovascular score explicitly assigns 1–2 points for lactate $\ge 5$ and $\ge 11$ mmol/L. Predictors and labels were extracted from the identical 0–24h window. | Reviewer 1 (Comment 4): Unanimous dominance of lactate is partly circular / diagnostic reconstruction (target leakage) rather than pure early predictive discovery. | Transparently document that Phoenix cardiovascular scoring directly incorporates serum lactate, and coagulation scoring incorporates platelets, INR, and D-dimer. Explain that their prominence reflects diagnostic concordance and organ dysfunction capturing during the initial 24h ICU window, rather than purely early predictive anticipation hours in advance. | No. Resolved by rigorous scientific disclosure. |
| **8** | Correlation statistics ($p < 10^{-98}$) presented as decisive evidence of explanation robustness and trust. (Section IV-C, Table III) | Spearman $\rho$ measures monotonicity of relative feature rankings across 225 features; $p$-values are asymptotic tests against the null hypothesis $\rho = 0$. | Reviewer 1 (Comments 1 & 4): High rank correlation does not prove that explanations are clinically faithful, reliable for individual patients, or decision-useful. Asymptotic $p$-values on $n=225$ features are trivially tiny. | Report 95% confidence intervals for Spearman correlations (e.g., CICU $\rho=0.9297$, 95% CI: [0.9095, 0.9455]; FedAvg vs FedProx $\rho=0.6889$, 95% CI: [0.6134, 0.7519]). Report top-$k$ overlap (80% top-5, 70% top-10). Explicitly state that rank correlation reflects global structural agreement, not individual patient fidelity or clinical actionability. | No. Computed from existing rankings. |
