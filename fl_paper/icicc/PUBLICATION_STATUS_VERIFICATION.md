## 3. Detailed Explanations

### What SSRN Inclusion Means
SSRN (Social Science Research Network) is a scholarly repository owned by Elsevier where researchers share working papers, preprints, and non-peer-reviewed conference collections. While SSRN assigns a citable DOI and provides high search visibility via Google Scholar:
- SSRN is **not** an indexed conference proceedings series like Springer LNNS or IEEE Xplore.
- Papers published exclusively on SSRN do not receive Scopus citation metrics or Web of Science indexing.
- Some conferences use SSRN to publish extended abstracts or companion proceedings for papers that did not meet the page threshold or score cutoff for the main Springer LNNS volume.
